Module 01

1. What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines programmed to think, reason, solve complex problems, and learn like humans.

Perceives surroundings & executes cognitive tasks
Mimics human decision making & logic processes
Domain: Computer Science
Module 02

2. What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning (ML) is a core subset of AI that allows software applications to become accurate at predicting outcomes without being explicitly programmed.

Learns directly from data patterns & experiences
Improves algorithm accuracy over time automatically
Domain: Data Science
Module 03

3. Where is AI used?

AI powers everyday technology around us! It touches healthcare, entertainment, transport, finance, and interactive communication worldwide.

Siri/Alexa, Voice Assistants & Spam filters
Netflix recommendations & Navigation apps
Domain: Applications
